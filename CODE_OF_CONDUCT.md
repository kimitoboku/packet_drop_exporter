# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free project community. Examples of positive behavior include constructive technical feedback, respect for different experience levels and communication styles, and a focus on the work rather than the person.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized language or imagery, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, publishing another person's private information, or personal attacks.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces. It also applies when someone is officially representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately where practical, and protect the privacy of reporters and affected people.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers using an available private contact method listed on the repository owner's profile. Do not publish sensitive details in an issue. Reports will be reviewed in good faith, but the project does not currently provide a response-time SLA.
